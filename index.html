Our Story

The Beginning

A Dream
over Coffee

It started with a simple idea — a cafe that stays open when the rest of the world closes. Kumar and Shaker, two friends with a shared love for great coffee and good company, opened Bean There to be that place. A refuge. A community. A home away from home.

The Philosophy

More Than
Just a Cafe

We believe a great cafe is one that makes you feel welcome at any hour. From morning chai to midnight cold brew — Bean There is open for it all. Every cup is brewed with care, every corner is lit with warmth.

Our World

Glimpses of

The Bean There Experience

"We didn't build a cafe. We built a feeling — one cup, one conversation, one late night at a time."

Kumar & Shaker, Founders

01

The Morning Ritual

Every great day begins with a great cup. Our baristas start before dawn, dialling in the perfect espresso. When you walk in for your first coffee, the world hasn't quite started yet — and that's the point.

02

The Afternoon Refuge

When the afternoon sun gets heavy and screens start to blur, Bean There is the reset. Grab a flat white, take a corner seat, and let the ambient playlist do the rest. Work better. Think clearer.

03

The Late Night

This is where Bean There truly comes alive. Midnight crowds, low lights, the smell of Maggi and coffee in the air. Whether you have a deadline or just want to watch the city slow down — we stay open for it all.

"The best conversations happen after midnight, over one more cup."
